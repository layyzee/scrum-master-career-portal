Section 44ADA 50% Tax Shield Architecture

Income Tax Act

How Indian professionals legally pay ~12–15% effective tax on global remote earnings

Total Tax Savings
₹4.5L – ₹7.5L+ Saved / Yr
1

50% Presumptive Expense

Under Section 44ADA, professionals earning up to ₹75 Lakhs gross receipts can declare flat 50% as business expenses (zero bills or receipts required). Tax is calculated strictly on the remaining 50%.

2

0% GST via LUT Export

Exporting software consultancy / agile management services to foreign clients qualifies as "Export of Services". By filing an online Letter of Undertaking (LUT), you pay 0% GST legally.

3

Bank FIRC Compliance

Each wire transfer from your foreign client arrives via Wise / Stripe / Swift with a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) verifying legitimate export income under RBI FEMA guidelines.
