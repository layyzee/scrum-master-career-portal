Section 44ADA 50% Tax Shield Architecture

Why hiring Balaji via international B2B contract is legally seamless, frictionless, and cost-effective.

Income Tax Act, 1961

50% Presumptive Expense

Under Section 44ADA, professional agile delivery consultants can treat 50% of gross foreign receipts (up to ₹75 Lakhs / ~$90k USD) as automated, audit-exempt operational expense, slashing effective tax liability.

Gross: $75,000 → Taxable: $37,500

0% GST via Letter of Undertaking

Software consulting provided to clients outside India qualifies as a zero-rated export of service under Indian GST law. Backed by annual Letter of Undertaking (LUT), invoices carry 0% GST with zero client overhead.

Form RFD-11 LUT Active • 0% Indirect Tax

Bank FIRC Compliance

Every cross-border wire (Wise, Stripe, direct SWIFT wire) automatically receives Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC) through Authorized Dealer Category-I banks (Citibank, HSBC, ICICI).
