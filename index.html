The Global Arbitrage

To a US/EU startup, paying $4,500/month is a fraction of a $140,000 domestic hire. To you in India, it is ~₹3,80,000/month (~₹45+ LPA) deposited straight into your bank via Wise or Deel.

2

Section 44ADA Presumptive Tax

Under Sec 44ADA, foreign technical consultants earning up to ₹75 Lakhs/yr are legally taxed on only 50% of their gross receipts. The other 50% is treated as standard presumptive business expense!

3

0% GST Export Status

By filing a free online GST LUT (Letter of Undertaking) on the GST portal, your foreign consulting services count as "Zero-Rated Export of Services"—meaning 0% GST liability!
