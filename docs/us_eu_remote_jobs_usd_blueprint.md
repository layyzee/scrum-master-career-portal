1. **The Math:** To a US/European startup, paying **\$3,500 – \$6,500/month** (\$42,000 – \$78,000/year) for an experienced Agile Delivery Lead / Scrum Master is a **bargain** (a US-based hire costs them \$130,000 – \$160,000 + benefits).

3. **The Indian Tax Loophole (Section 44ADA):** Foreign contractors in India can file under **Section 44ADA (Presumptive Taxation for Professionals)**. If you earn up to ₹75 Lakhs/year as a technical consultant:
   * The Indian government presumes **50% of your gross earnings are business expenses**.
   * You only pay tax on the remaining 50%.
   * On ₹50 Lakhs gross, you are only taxed on ₹25 Lakhs, dropping your effective tax rate to ~12%–14% (compared to 30%+ TDS for corporate salaried employees). With a free GST LUT (Letter of Undertaking), export of services has **0% GST**.

### 3. The "Deel / Contractor" LinkedIn Boolean Search Formula
When searching LinkedIn from India, LinkedIn default algorithms push domestic Indian jobs. To surface US/EU companies looking for international contractors, use these exact Boolean searches:

#### Search A: Finding US/EU Startups Hiring via Deel or Global Contractors
1. Go to **LinkedIn Jobs**.
2. Location: **United States** (or **Worldwide**).
3. Filter **Remote** = `On`.
4. Filter **Job Type** = `Contract` (This strips out W-2 US-citizenship-only jobs).
5. In the Search Bar, paste:
```text
("Scrum Master" OR "Agile Delivery" OR "Technical Project Manager") AND ("Deel" OR "remote contractor" OR "worldwide" OR "anywhere" OR "international") NOT ("US only" OR "must reside in the US" OR "W2 only")
```

#### Search B: Google "X-Ray" Sourcing (Bypasses LinkedIn Restrictions)
Paste this directly into Google:
```text
site:linkedin.com/jobs/view ("Scrum Master" OR "Agile Delivery") ("Deel" OR "contractor" OR "worldwide") -"US only" -"must be a US Citizen"
```
