# The Startup & Hidden Job Market Playbook for Scrum Masters / Agile Delivery Leads

## 1. Why Startups Have "Hidden" Agile Opportunities
In high-growth startups (10 to 50 engineers), founders and CTOs rarely post a public listing titled **"Scrum Master"** because:
1. **Misconception of Overhead:** Early-stage leaders fear enterprise "agile bureaucracy" or ceremonial overhead.
2. **The Real Pain Point:** Founders, Product Heads, or Engineering Leads are drowning in delivery chaos—missed sprint dates, unrefined backlogs, unclear ticket acceptance criteria, and engineers blocked waiting for QA/DevOps sync.
3. **Disguised Titles:** The exact work of a Scrum Master in startups is usually posted under alternative titles:
   * **Agile Delivery Lead / Delivery Manager**
   * **Founding Agile Lead**
   * **Technical Product Owner (TPO) / Sprint Lead**
   * **Engineering Operations (EngOps) Lead**
   * **Product Operations (ProdOps) Manager**

## 3. The "Trigger Event" Sourcing Method
The most reliable way to find unadvertised roles is catching startups during an **engineering scaling inflection point**:

### Step 1: Track Freshly Funded Startups
Monitor weekly funding rounds on:
* **Inc42** (Indian Startup Funding Roundup)
* **YourStory** (Funding News)
* **Entrackr** / **Tracxn**

### Step 2: The Inflection Point
When a startup raises **Seed ($1M–$3M)** or **Series A ($4M–$12M)**, their immediate mandate is to expand from 1 small dev team (5–8 engineers) to 3–4 parallel squads (20–35 engineers). 
* At this stage, the Founder/CTO can no longer run daily stand-ups and sprint planning themselves.
* **They need a Scrum Master immediately, but haven't written the job description yet.**
